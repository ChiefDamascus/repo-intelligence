# Contributing to repo-intelligence

Thank you for considering a contribution. Before you start, please read the contributing terms below and the project license.

## License

This project's original code is available under the **Zero-Clause BSD license (0BSD)**. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the complete license text.

By making a contribution, you represent that your contribution is compatible with the 0BSD license and that you have the right to make it under those terms.

- Submit original contributions under 0BSD. Identify third-party material and its license separately.
- Third-party code, documentation, assets, or other material that you include remains subject to its own license. You must identify the material and any required notices.

## Contribution rules

- **Submit only material you are authorized to contribute under the project's license.** Identify third-party code, documentation, assets, and required notices. Do not submit confidential information or material obtained or generated in breach of an applicable agreement.
- **For AI-assisted contributions**, record the tool or service used and confirm that its applicable terms permit the contribution.

## How to contribute

- Keep changes focused on the recorded approved scope for the milestone you are working on.
- Follow the existing project style and conventions.
- Preserve the existing project tests and add new tests that cover your changes.
- Document any user-visible or behavioral change in the relevant place (`README.md`, `PLAN.md`, or the applicable phase documentation).
- Report your changes, evidence, and any limitations before request/review; this project requires independent Codex review before acceptance.
